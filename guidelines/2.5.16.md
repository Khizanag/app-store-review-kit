# 2.5.16

Source: [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#2.5.16), last updated 2026-06-08.

Widgets, extensions, and notifications should be related to the content and functionality of your app.

- **(a)** Additionally, all App Clip features and functionality must be included in the main app binary. App Clips cannot contain advertising.
